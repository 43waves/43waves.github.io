Ads Revenue Viewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Overview

Ads Revenue Viewer ("the App") is developed by 43waves. The App is a read-only viewer for your own Google AdMob revenue data. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

What We Don't Collect

  • No servers of our own - We don't operate any backend. Your data flows directly between your device and Google.
  • No personal information sent to us - We don't receive your name, email, or AdMob data on any server we control
  • No tracking by us - We don't track your behavior or location
  • No analytics - We don't use any analytics or tracking services

What Stays On Your Device

  • Cached AdMob account info - Your AdMob publisher account details (publisher ID, reporting time zone, currency, account name) are cached locally so the App can show your last-known state without re-fetching
  • Theme preference - Your selected theme (System/Light/Dark)
  • Onboarding status - Whether you've completed initial setup
  • Ad cooldown - Timestamp to limit ad frequency

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by signing out, uninstalling the App, or clearing app data.

Google Sign-In and AdMob Access

The App requires you to sign in with the Google account that owns your AdMob publisher account so it can fetch your revenue data on your behalf.

What the App Requests

  • Google profile - Your name, email address, and profile picture, provided by Google Sign-In so the App can show which account is signed in
  • AdMob read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly) - Used to read your AdMob publisher account info and revenue metrics. The App cannot create, modify, or delete anything in your AdMob account.

How Your AdMob Data Is Used

Data fetched from AdMob is used solely to display your revenue on your device. In line with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, the App:

  • Uses your AdMob data only to provide the revenue-viewing feature you requested
  • Does not transfer your AdMob data to any third party
  • Does not use your AdMob data for advertising, including retargeting or serving personalized ads

Signing Out

Signing out from within the App disconnects your Google account and clears the App's cached account data on your device. You can also revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and opt-out options at Google's Ads Settings.

Ad Frequency

  • Ads are shown when opening the App (cold start) or returning to it (resume)
  • A cooldown period ensures you won't see ads too frequently

Permissions

Internet

Required to sign in with Google, fetch your AdMob revenue data, and display advertisements. No personal data is transmitted to any server operated by 43waves.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for AdMob account holders and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising shown is handled by Google AdMob, which has its own policies regarding personalized advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
