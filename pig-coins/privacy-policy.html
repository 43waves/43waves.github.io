Pig Coins Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Pig Coins ("the App") is developed by 43waves. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information - We don't collect names, emails, or account data
  • No usage data sent to our servers - We don't send your gameplay data to any server
  • No tracking by us - We don't track your behavior or location

What Stays On Your Device

  • High score - Your best score

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and opt-out options at Google's Ads Settings.

Ads may be shown when you open the App or come back to it.

Unity

The App is made with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect basic information about your device and how the App runs, such as the device model, Android version and any crashes, to keep its engine working well. We don't use Unity Analytics. Unity handles this information as described in Unity's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Advertising ID

Google AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to show ads. You can reset it, or turn off personalized ads, in your device's settings.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The advertising shown is handled by Google AdMob, which has its own policies regarding personalized advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at 43waves@gmail.com.
