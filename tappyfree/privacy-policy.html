Tappy Free Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

Tappy Free ("the App") is a simple tap counter developed by 43waves. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data whatsoever. It has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data Collection

This App collects nothing. Full stop.

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information - We don't collect names, emails, or account data
  • No usage data - Nothing is sent to us or anyone else; the App has no servers and no internet access
  • No tracking - We don't track your behavior or location
  • No analytics - We don't use any analytics or tracking services
  • No advertising - The App does not display ads and does not use any ad networks
  • No third-party sharing - There is no data to share, and the App does not integrate with any third-party services

What Stays On Your Device

  • Counter value - Your current tap count

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Permissions

The App does not request any special device permissions. It does not access the internet, your files, your location, or any other device features beyond displaying its own screen.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at 43waves@gmail.com.
