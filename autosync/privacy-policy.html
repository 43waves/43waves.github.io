Autosync Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Autosync ("the App") is developed by 43waves. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information - We don't collect names, emails, or account data
  • No usage data sent to servers - Your sync preferences stay on your device
  • No tracking - We don't track your behavior or location

What Stays On Your Device

  • Sync mode - The sync mode you've chosen
  • Sync schedule - Your sync interval, times and conditions (such as Wi-Fi only or while charging)
  • Battery saver setting - Whether to keep syncing while battery saver is on
  • Theme preference - Your selected theme (System/Light/Dark)
  • Onboarding status - Whether you've completed initial setup
  • Ad cooldown - Timestamp to limit ad frequency

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Permissions

Notifications

Used to show a notification with the current sync status while the App is working, with a quick way to turn sync on or off.

Internet

Used only for displaying advertisements. No personal data is transmitted by the App itself.

Network and Wi-Fi Status

Used to check whether you're connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data, for the Wi-Fi sync mode.

Sync Settings

Used to turn your phone's auto-sync setting on and off, based on the conditions you choose. This is the core functionality of the App.

Run at Startup

Used to keep your sync schedule working after your phone restarts.

Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data for personalized advertising as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The App is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at 43waves@gmail.com.
