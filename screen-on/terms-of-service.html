What the App Does

The App keeps your screen awake by changing your device's screen timeout setting, and by holding the screen on while "never sleep" is turned on. It can restore your previous timeout, either when you turn it off or when a timer ends.

  • The App needs the "Modify system settings" permission to do this. You can remove it at any time in your device's settings.
  • Some phones limit or undo screen timeout changes, or stop apps running in the background to save battery. The App tries to work around this and explains how to set its battery use to Unrestricted, but we can't promise it will work the same way on every device.
  • Keeping the screen on uses more battery and may, over long periods, cause screen burn-in on some displays. You decide when and how long to use it.
