Weshare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Weshare ("the App") lets you share your live location on a map with the people you invite, developed by 43waves. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what the App stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Your Account

The App has no sign-up screen. When you first open it, it creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to create it.

Data Collection

What Is Stored on Our Server

To share your location with other people, the App stores the following in a Google Firebase Realtime Database (hosted by Google in the United States):

  • Display name - The name you choose, so the people you share with know who you are
  • Profile photo (optional) - A small copy (at most 160 x 160 pixels) of a photo you choose
  • Live location - While you are sharing: your latest position, its accuracy, your speed and the time it was taken. Only the latest position is kept; there is no location history
  • Meeting point (optional) - The name and position of a meeting point you set
  • Saved places - Your 20 latest meeting points, backed up so a reinstall brings them back
  • People you share with - Links between your anonymous account and the accounts of the people you've connected with, and which of them you share your location with
  • Invite codes - A code you create to invite someone, which expires after 24 hours and works once
  • Recovery record - A one-way hash of your phone's Android device identifier, so reinstalling the App on the same phone brings your account back. The identifier itself is never stored or uploaded

What We Don't Collect

  • No contact details - We don't collect emails, phone numbers or your phone's contact list
  • No location history - Each new position replaces the previous one
  • No analytics - We don't use any analytics or tracking services
  • No selling of data - We don't sell or share your data with anyone except as described in this policy

What Stays On Your Device

  • Settings - Your theme, units and arrival notification preference
  • Map choices - Who the map shows and follows
  • Saved places - A copy of your saved meeting points
  • Photos of your people - Downloaded once and kept so they aren't fetched again
  • Ad timing - Whether you have started sharing yet and when the last ad was shown, to limit ads

This data can be cleared by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. It is never included in Android backups.

Who Can See Your Data

  • Your location - Only the people you have chosen to share with. You can stop sharing with one person, or with everyone, at any time. Stopping sharing with everyone deletes your location from the server
  • Your name, photo and meeting point - Only people you have connected with through an invite
  • Your saved places and recovery record - Only you; no other user can see them

Access is enforced by the database's security rules. We don't look at your data except when needed to keep the service running or to answer a request from you.

Location

Location is the App's core feature. The App reads your device's location to show you on the map and, while you are sharing, to send your latest position to the people you share with. While you are sharing, the App keeps updating your location in the background - including with the screen off or the App closed - and always shows a notification while it does. Sharing stops when you turn it off.

Arrival estimates and arrival notifications ("Sam has arrived") are worked out on your device from the positions it already receives; nothing extra is stored for them.

Map and Place Search

The App uses OpenStreetMap services to show the map and find places:

  • Map tiles - Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area you're viewing
  • Place search - When you search for a meeting point, your search text, along with a broad area around your location (to rank nearby places higher), is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service. Your exact coordinates are not sent

These requests are handled by the OpenStreetMap Foundation as described in its Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The App displays app-open advertisements powered by Google AdMob. The App sends no personal data with its ad requests, but when ads are shown, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and opt-out options at Google's Ads Settings. Ads are never shown while you are sharing your location.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Delete your account - Settings > Start fresh deletes your account and all of its data from the server, including your links with other people
  • Inactive accounts - An account that hasn't opened the App for 90 days is deleted automatically, with all of its data
  • Meeting points - A meeting point set more than 30 days ago is removed automatically
  • Invites - Expired invite codes are deleted automatically

Uninstalling the App does not delete your account straight away, so a reinstall on the same phone can bring it back; it is deleted after 90 days of inactivity. To have it deleted sooner, use Start fresh before uninstalling, or contact us.

Permissions

Location

Used to show where you are on the map and, while you are sharing, to send your position to the people you share with. See the Location section above.

Foreground Service

Used to keep sharing your location while the screen is off or the App is in the background, with a visible notification.

Notifications

Used to show the ongoing sharing notification and to tell you when someone arrives at your meeting point.

Internet Access

Used to share locations with your people, load map tiles, search places and load advertisements.

Choosing a Profile Photo

When you choose a profile photo, the App opens your device's system photo picker. The App only receives the photo you select, and does not browse, read or upload any of your other photos or files.

Sharing Invites

Invites are sent through your device's share sheet, using an app you choose. The App only shares the invite message; it does not read your contacts or messages.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The advertising shown is handled by Google AdMob, which has its own policies regarding personalized advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
