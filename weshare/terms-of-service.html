What the App Does

The App lets you share your live location on a map with people you invite, set a meeting point, and see how far away the people sharing with you are and when they might arrive.

  • Locations depend on your phone's GPS and network, and on the other person's. A position can be late, out of date or off by some distance, and arrival times are estimates worked out from recent movement.
