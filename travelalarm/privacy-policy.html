Travel Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

Travel Alarm ("the App") is a location-based alarm developed by 43waves: search a destination and get woken up when you're within 2 km of it. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information - We don't collect names, emails, or account data
  • No usage data sent to our servers - We don't operate any servers and the App has no accounts
  • No location storage - Your location is used on your device in real time and is never saved or uploaded by us
  • No analytics - We don't use any analytics or tracking services
  • No advertising - The App does not display ads and does not use any ad networks

The App does not save any settings or history. Your destination and trip are kept only in memory while the App is open and are cleared as soon as you close it or the trip ends.

Location

Location is the App's core feature: while a trip is running, the App reads your device's location and compares it on your device against your chosen destination to ring the alarm when you're within 2 km. Your location:

  • Is only accessed while the App is open and a trip is running
  • Is never accessed in the background - the App has no background service and stops tracking your location once it is closed
  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is never stored, logged, or sent to us

Map and Destination Search

The App uses OpenStreetMap services to show the map and find destinations:

  • Map tiles - Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area you're viewing
  • Destination search - Your search text, along with a broad area around your location (to rank nearby places higher), is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service. Your exact coordinates are not sent

These requests are handled by the OpenStreetMap Foundation as described in its Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Location

Used to measure your distance to the destination and ring the alarm when you're within range. See the Location section above - your location never leaves your device, and is only used while the App is open.

Internet Access

Used to load map tiles and search destinations. The App does not transmit your settings or location over the network.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
